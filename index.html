01 About Me

Curious about how things work — and how to build them better.

I'm Rahul Mali, a Computer Science Engineering student at NB Navale Sinhgad College of Engineering, Solapur. I enjoy turning ideas into responsive, accessible web experiences and understanding what happens beyond the interface — APIs, databases, deployment, and the cloud.
